# Third-party assets

The repository's Boost Software License covers the portfolio source code. The following assets retain their own terms:

- Display fonts: original Rengok and Brogetta binaries and supplied notices in `assets/fonts/`.
- Technology and social icons: upstream licences, attribution, source revisions and brand information in `public/icons/`.
- Personal résumés, project screenshots and walkthroughs: supplied by the portfolio owner for this website. They are not licensed for unrelated reuse by the source-code licence.

The source and checksum inventories in `public/asset-manifest.json`, `public/icons/icon-manifest.json` and `public/media/video-manifest.json` preserve provenance. Source recordings and design exports are archived separately; they are not needed to build or host the site.
